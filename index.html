Key Investment & Technical Highlights

Zero Grid-Access Bottleneck

Strictly configured as Co-located GREEN (charging exclusively from solar surplus and curtailment mitigation), requiring zero grid-import capacity under RD 1183/2020.

Engineering Ready-to-File

Preliminary engineering designs (Anteproyectos) completed in November 2026 for both assets; secured land footprint ready for containerized BESS deployment.

SME (PYME) Grant Advantage

Project SPVs qualify as Spanish SMEs (PYME), unlocking a +20% bonus intensity in upcoming IDAE / FEDER storage subsidy calls.

Target Partnership Structures

1. Investors / IPPs / ESCO

Battery-as-a-Service (BaaS), ESCO (100% partner-funded CAPEX), SPV Joint Venture, Tolling Agreement, or Project Finance / Mezzanine (~4.15 M€).

2. BESS OEMs & EPCs

Supply of 4h LFP containers + PCS certified under NTS 2.1 / P.O. 12.2 (0 kW grid-import lock), fixed-price Turnkey EPC, or Vendor Finance.

3. RTM Traders & Optimizers

Route-to-Market (RTM) agreements under Floor Price + Profit Share combining wholesale arbitrage (OMIE / IDC) and REE balancing (aFRR / mFRR).

4. O&M Service Providers

Integrated PV + BESS O&M contract with local field teams in Granada and Albacete (SLA < 2–4 h), 20 kV MV maintenance, and herbicide-free vegetation control.
